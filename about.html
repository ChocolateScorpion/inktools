About

Built for privacy.
Designed for simplicity.

InkTools started from a real problem: iPhone photos in HEIC format often don't open on Windows or other devices, and most converters require uploading your files to someone else's server.

That felt wrong. Your photos are yours. They shouldn't have to travel across the internet just to change format.

So InkTools was built differently — every tool runs entirely in your browser, using your device's own processing power. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is stored. Nothing leaves your hands.

What we stand for

Privacy first
Files never leave your device. No servers, no storage, no risk.
Instant results
No waiting, no queues. Processing happens as fast as your device allows.
No account needed
Open a tool, use it, done. No sign-ups, no emails, no friction.
Always free
Every tool is free to use. No limits, no paywalls, no hidden costs.

InkTools is an independent project, built and maintained by a small team. We're constantly adding new tools — if there's something you'd like to see, we'd love to hear from you.
