What does HEIC to MP4 mean?

HEIC is the photo format iPhones save by default. MP4 is the universal video format that plays on every device, browser, and app. This tool takes your HEIC photos and turns them into a slideshow video — each photo displayed in sequence, ready to share or post anywhere.

How to use this tool

  • Drop or choose your HEIC files (up to 30 photos)
  • Set how long each photo should appear (1–8 seconds)
  • Pick your output resolution and frame rate
  • Click Create video — it runs in your browser, no upload needed
  • Download the finished MP4 file

Is this private?

Completely. Your photos never leave your device — all the encoding happens using your browser's own video tools (the Canvas and MediaRecorder APIs). We never receive your files, and nothing is stored on any server.

What video format will I get?

The tool outputs the best format your browser supports: MP4 (H.264) on Safari and iOS, WebM (VP8) on Chrome and Firefox. Both formats play on all modern devices. The download button shows you which format was produced.
