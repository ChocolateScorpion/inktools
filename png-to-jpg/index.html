FAQ

Will I lose quality converting PNG to JPG?

JPG uses lossy compression, so there's a slight quality reduction. At the high quality setting this tool uses by default, the difference is barely noticeable, and the file size savings are significant.

What happens to transparent areas?

Since JPG doesn't support transparency, transparent areas in your PNG are filled with a white background during conversion.

How much smaller will my JPG be?

Typically 60–80% smaller than the original PNG, depending on the image content.

Can I convert PNG logos to JPG?

You can, but logos often have transparency and sharp edges that JPG compresses poorly. For logos, keep PNG or convert to SVG instead.

Is this tool secure?

Yes. Your images are processed locally in your browser and never uploaded to any server.
