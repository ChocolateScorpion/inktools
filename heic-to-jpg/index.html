What this tool does

This tool allows you to convert HEIC images into JPG format directly in your browser. HEIC is commonly used by iPhones, but it is not always supported on all devices or platforms. With this converter, you can quickly transform your images into a widely compatible format without installing any software.

How to use

  1. Upload or drag and drop your HEIC file
  2. Wait for the preview to appear
  3. Click the convert button
  4. Download your JPG image instantly

Benefits

  • High-quality JPG output

Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert HEIC to JPG?

JPG is a widely supported format that works across devices, browsers, and platforms, making it easier to share and use your images.

Is this tool secure?

Yes. Your files are processed locally in your browser whenever possible, which means they are not uploaded or stored on any server.

Will I lose quality?

JPG uses compression, but this tool maintains high image quality during the conversion process.
