What this tool does

This tool converts HEIC photos into JPG directly in your browser — no upload, no account. HEIC is Apple's default photo format, roughly half the size of an equivalent JPG at the same quality, but it isn't supported everywhere: Windows without the Apple codec, most Android apps, older editing software, and many websites reject it outright. JPG works on virtually everything, which is why converting is usually the fastest fix.

FAQ

Why convert HEIC to JPG?

iPhones shoot in HEIC by default, but many apps, websites, and Windows PCs don't support it. JPG works everywhere.

Does converting lose quality?

JPG uses lossy compression, so there's a small amount of quality loss compared to the original HEIC. At the high quality setting this tool uses by default, the difference is invisible to the naked eye.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes — drop or select up to 20 HEIC files and they'll all convert together in one click.

Will my photos look different after conversion?

Colors, exposure, and composition are preserved. The main difference is file size — JPG typically runs 10–20% larger than HEIC at equivalent quality.

How do I know if my iPhone photos are in HEIC?

If your iPhone camera is set to "High Efficiency" (Settings → Camera → Formats), it's shooting HEIC. You can also confirm by checking the file extension.

Is my file safe?

Yes. Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
