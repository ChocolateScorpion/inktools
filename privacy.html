The short version: InkTools processes all images locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never shared with anyone. Period.

1. Image processing

All image conversion, compression, and resizing operations performed by InkTools happen entirely within your browser using standard web technologies (JavaScript, Canvas API).

Your image files are never transmitted to any external server. They are loaded into memory temporarily for processing and are cleared when you close or navigate away from the page.

2. Data we collect

InkTools does not collect any personal information during normal tool use. The only exception is when you voluntarily contact us via the contact form — in that case, your name and email are transmitted to Formspree to deliver your message to us.

We do not ask for your name, email, or any identifying details to use any of our tools.

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • General geographic region (country level)
  • Device type and browser version
  • How visitors arrive at the site (search, direct, etc.)

This data is anonymous, aggregated, and used only to understand how people use InkTools so we can improve it. No individual users are identified or tracked.

3. Cookies

InkTools uses minimal cookies. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors and sessions. These cookies do not contain any personally identifiable information.

We also display ads through Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing activity. You can opt out of personalized ads through Google's ad settings.

4. Advertising

InkTools is supported by Google AdSense. Ads may be displayed on our pages. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to InkTools or other websites.

InkTools does not control the content of ads displayed by Google and is not responsible for their privacy practices. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

InkTools uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics
  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Formspree — contact form delivery. When you use the contact form, your name and email are submitted to Formspree's servers to forward your message. See their privacy policy.
  • heic2any — open-source library for HEIC conversion, runs entirely in the browser

None of these services receive your image files. Only anonymous analytics and ad-related data is shared with Google services. Formspree only receives the information you voluntarily submit through the contact form.

6. Children's privacy

InkTools does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Our tools are general-purpose utilities not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of InkTools after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please get in touch with us.
