What is InkTools?

InkTools is a collection of free online image utilities designed to help you convert, compress, and resize images directly in your browser. No account, no upload, no waiting.

All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring fast performance and complete privacy.

Why Use InkTools

  • No uploads required — your files stay on your device
  • Fast and simple image conversion
  • Supports HEIC, JPG, and PNG formats
  • Reduce image file size without losing quality
  • Works on desktop and mobile devices
  • 100% free to use

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert HEIC images to JPG?

Yes. Convert HEIC files from iPhone to JPG format for better compatibility with most devices and platforms.

Do I need to upload my images?

No. All tools work directly in your browser — your images are never uploaded or stored anywhere.

Can I compress images without losing quality?

Yes. The image compressor reduces file size while maintaining good visual quality, making it ideal for web use.

What formats are supported?

InkTools supports HEIC, JPG, and PNG formats, including conversions between them.

Is InkTools free?

Yes, all tools are completely free to use with no hidden costs.
