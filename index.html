Frequently asked questions
HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default format used by iPhones. Windows doesn't support it natively unless you install a paid Microsoft codec. Converting to JPG is the simplest fix — it opens everywhere without any extra software.
Your photos never leave your device. All conversion happens inside your browser using JavaScript — there's no upload, no server, no storage. We have nothing to store because we never receive your files.
JPG uses compression to reduce file size — ideal for photos you want to share, email, or post on social media. PNG is lossless (no quality loss) and supports transparency — better for screenshots, graphics, or images you'll edit further. When in doubt, JPG is the right choice for iPhone photos.
InkTools supports batch conversion — you can drop up to 20 files at once and download them all in one click. The limit exists to keep the conversion fast on all devices, including phones.
Yes. InkTools runs in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge — on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Since everything happens in the browser, there's nothing to install.
Completely free — no hidden costs, no account required, no file limits beyond batch caps. InkTools is supported by ads (the ones you see on these pages). That's the whole model. No subscriptions, no upsells.
