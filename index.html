What is this tool?

InkTools is a collection of free online image utilities designed to help you convert, compress, and resize images directly in your browser.

You can convert HEIC to JPG or PNG, compress images to reduce file size, resize photos, or switch between JPG and PNG formats — all without uploading your files.

All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring fast performance and complete privacy.

How It Works
